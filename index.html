FlowPay

A digital wallet and UPI-style payments app, inspired by Google Pay and PhonePe

I built FlowPay end to end to show how I handle the parts of a payments app that can't go wrong: money moves atomically, every rupee is accounted for in a double-entry ledger, and a payment retried on a bad network is never charged twice.

  • OTP sign-in with JWT sessions
  • Real-time wallet balance
  • Peer-to-peer transfers with atomic transactions
  • Double-entry bookkeeping ledger
  • Idempotency-safe payments
  • Bank account linking
  • Scan and pay with QR codes
  • Transaction history
